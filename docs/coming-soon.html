Cable parking

No module is out of reach

Leave a cable end in a hole on the bar at the left, scroll the rack as far as you need, then pull it out onto the jack you wanted. Compatible jacks light up while you aim.

Each hole keeps the name of the module and output that end came from — after the third cable you no longer remember.

Selection

Pick up half the rack and move it

Tap the modules you want, tap again to let them go. Then hold and drag: they travel together, cables following.

While you choose, knobs and jacks stay put — no value nudged by accident in the middle of tidying up.

At a glance

See what is in your hand

Chosen modules get a red halo around them. The panel stays readable — knobs, labels and cables are all still there while you work.

Room

Clear space in one gesture

The bin takes away everything you picked. It tells you how many modules are going and asks first; change your mind and undo is right beside it.

Copy and paste sit next to it, so a corner of the patch that works can be repeated wherever you like.
